# The AI Agency Blueprint Signs AI Audit Agreement With a New Jersey Municipality

[CITY, NJ, DATE] - The AI Agency Blueprint has signed an agreement with [client name pending approval], a New Jersey municipality, to carry out an AI Audit of how the municipality handles routine resident questions. The agreement was signed on [DATE]. It covers the audit, and no system build is part of it.

The AI Audit looks at one workflow: the municipality's handling of routine resident questions that arrive by email and by phone. The audit maps how those questions are received and answered today. It then documents what a customer response system would handle and what it would not handle. All of this is written down before anything is built.

The audit takes seven business days. At the end, the municipality will have a written record of the workflow and of where an automated response system would and would not fit. The agreement does not commit the municipality to any later work.

"The audit puts in writing what a response system would and would not handle, before anything is built," said Joaquin Garcia, CEO of The AI Agency Blueprint. [QUOTE PENDING approval: Joaquin Garcia]

Mr. Garcia is the company's spokesperson on this agreement. No statement from the municipality is included in this release, because the municipality has not approved being named or quoted.
