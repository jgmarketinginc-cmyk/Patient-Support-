# Quick-Reference Card: What the System Does and Does Not Do

The AI Agency Blueprint | Customer Response Agent for your business | TEMPLATE, shadow draft, not shared

## What it does (five steps)
1. A question arrives by email, phone or portal.
2. The system checks it against the documents your business provided and approved (for example lease or policy terms, portal steps, office hours).
3. If the answer is in those documents, the response goes out.
4. If the question is outside those documents, or urgent, it goes to a person: the named human operator and the office.
5. A person reviews what the system could not answer.

## What it does not do
- Answer from anything other than your approved documents.
- Handle urgent questions. Urgent goes to a person.
- Make judgment calls.
- Replace the named human operator or the office.
- Do inspections.
- Do scheduling.
- Do work-order handoff.
- Do document intake.
- Connect to other platforms or systems.
- Do open-request tracking.
- Anything beyond the five steps above. If it is not in this training library, it is not part of your system: ask us.

## Quick rules
| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Answer is in the approved documents | The system responds |
| Answer is not in the approved documents | A person takes it |
| The question is urgent | A person takes it |
| The system could not answer | A person reviews it |
