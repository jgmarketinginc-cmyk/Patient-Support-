This proposal is about one workflow: answering tenant questions. That is the only thing it covers.

## 2. What the system is, and how it protects against a wrong answer

You asked: "How would it know the office hours are right? That's the thing that burned me." Here is the plain answer.

- It answers only from documents you give us and you approve: your lease terms, your portal steps, your office hours.
- Anything outside those documents goes to a person.
- Urgent items go to a person too, so they are not left in a queue.
- A named human operator reviews what the system could not answer.

The office hours are right because you supplied and approved them. The system does not guess.

We are a new company. We have no client results to show you yet, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. What we can offer is a clear description of how we build and a low-risk way to start.

## 3. Your three options

| | Option 1 | Option 2 | Option 3 |

**Option 1: AI Audit ($1,500).** We map what comes in and what the system would handle, before any build. It is the low-risk first step, and you told us on the call that this is one you can decide on. The Audit fee is not credited toward the Install.

**Option 2: AI Operations Install ($18,000). This is the one we recommend.** It matches the outcome you described. We build a customer response system for tenant questions, trained on your own documents, with a named human operator, where anything outside your approved documents goes to a person.

**Option 3: Install + Managed Operations ($18,000 + $2,400 per month).** This is Option 2 plus an optional monthly retainer for the one workflow in scope. The retainer is optional and we assume nothing monthly unless you agree to it. To be plain: Option 3 does not pay back on labor savings alone (numbers in section 4). Its case rests on things we have not priced, such as operator oversight and urgent items not getting buried. If the monthly cost is not worth it to you, Option 2 stands on its own.

We do not discount. If the Install is too large a step, the way to reduce it is to start with Option 1.
