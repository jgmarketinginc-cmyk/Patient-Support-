## Content rules (SET by the agent file, confirm with Joaquin)

- Reference and translation on every quoted verse. Quotation marks only around exact text.
- No guarantees of healing, wealth, protection or success. No fear tactics, no attacks on denominations, people or politics.
- No commercial pitch inside scripture content unless Joaquin asks for one.
- Spoken length 15 to 60 seconds; hook in the first line.
